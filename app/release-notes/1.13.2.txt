Music that starts, or says why it does not
- A song you tap shows at once that it is loading, and you are told when the network is slow.
- With no network, or a song YouTube will not play, you are told right away. A song that cannot be played is skipped.
- Seeking on a weak or lost connection no longer closes the app.
- After a STOP button on headphones, a car or a watch, Play starts the music again.
- The player at the bottom no longer breaks when you tap songs quickly.
- On a slow network songs start sooner.
- In a room you are told when this phone could not load the song, and Play loads it again.
