Songs load when one server is down
- A song's address names a server of your internet provider, and on some connections one of them does not answer. The app used to wait 15 seconds for it, which showed "Slow connection, still loading" or left a song out of "Download all". Now it gives up on that server after 5 seconds and reads the same song from the next one, and it remembers the one that failed for a few minutes.
- Playing, downloading and video all use this, on Android and iPhone.
